MitFloww Review

Review
★★★★☆ 4.5/5 • Early-stage product

A practical breakdown of whether MitFloww actually helps freelancers get paid before delivering work.

Key Idea

Clients only get full access after payment — eliminating the risk of unpaid work.

Overview

MitFloww is a secure file sharing platform designed for freelancers. Instead of sending full files upfront, clients only gain access after completing payment.

Pros

✔ Ensures payment before delivery
✔ Reduces client ghosting
✔ Simple workflow, no complex setup

Cons

✖ Still early-stage product
✖ Requires client understanding initially

Compared to normal workflow

Typical workflow

Send files → chase payment

With MitFloww

Preview → payment → access

Final Verdict

Worth it for freelancers?

If you frequently face payment issues after delivery, MitFloww provides a simple and effective way to enforce a payment-first workflow without relying on trust.
