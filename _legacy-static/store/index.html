For creators

Your page and the store work together.

You keep a personal asset page that you customise and share directly. The store is an extra place to be found. It does not replace your own page.

  1. 01

    Your own asset page

    Customise it, share it with clients and followers, and keep your own presentation.

  2. 02

    Listed in the store

    Eligible assets can also appear in the MitFloww Assets Store next to work from other creators.

  3. 03

    More ways to be found

    People who don't know you yet can discover your work by category and search.
