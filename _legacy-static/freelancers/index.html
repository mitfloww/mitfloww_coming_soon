How discovery works

Skill first, then distance.

  1. 01

    Pick the skill

    Filter by specialty or search for the exact thing you need, like packaging or colour grading.

  2. 02

    Choose your city

    Freelancers in your city appear first, then others in your state, then people who work remotely.

  3. 03

    See the work

    Open a profile to see their portfolio, service area and specialties before you get in touch.
