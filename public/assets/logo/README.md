# MitFloww Social Media Bundle

These are the MitFloww combined logos (original icon + v2 bold wordmark), in the four colour versions you asked for, each in a horizontal and a vertical layout.

| # | Version | Icon | Wordmark | File name part | Use on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full black | `#000000` | `#000000` | `-black` | Light backgrounds and photos |
| 2 | Full white | `#FFFFFF` | `#FFFFFF` | `-white` | Dark backgrounds and photos |
| 3 | Brand icon + white wordmark | `#005BDD` + `#00C7FF` | `#FFFFFF` | `-color-white-wordmark` | Dark backgrounds (navy, black, dark photos) |
| 4 | Brand icon + black wordmark | `#005BDD` + `#00C7FF` | `#000000` | `-color-black-wordmark` | Light backgrounds (white, light grey, light photos) |

**Layouts:**
- `horizontal`: icon to the left of the wordmark. Best for banners, headers and wide posts.
- `vertical`: icon above the wordmark. Best for square posts, profile pictures and stories.

## Which file to use

| Need | File |
|---|---|
| **Profile picture** (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, YouTube) | `Ready-to-post-1080/vertical/…-1080x1080.png`. The vertical layout reads best in a small circle. |
| Square post or "logo card" | `Ready-to-post-1080/vertical/` or `/horizontal/` |
| Logo on top of your own photo or graphic | `PNG-transparent/…-2048.png` (or `-4096.png` for large banners) |
| Cover / banner images (LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Facebook) | Place `PNG-transparent/horizontal/…-2048.png` onto your banner design |
| Story / Reel end card | `PNG-transparent/vertical/…-1080.png` on your background |
| Agencies, printers, motion designers | `SVG/` or `PDF/` |

**Ready-to-post backgrounds:**
- Full black and brand icon + black wordmark: white `#FFFFFF`.
- Full white and brand icon + white wordmark: MitFloww navy `#001233`.

Every square keeps the whole logo inside the circular crop that platforms apply to profile pictures. The logo fits inside a centred circle of 80% of the width, so it stays at least 108 px from every edge.

**Transparent PNG sizes (width × height):**
- Horizontal: 1080 × 203, 2048 × 386, 4096 × 771
- Vertical: 1080 × 564, 2048 × 1070, 4096 × 2140

## Notes

- **Same artwork as v2.** Shapes, letter spacing and icon-to-wordmark spacing are the same as in `MitFloww-Logo-Bundle-v2`. Only the colours differ.
- **The i-dot follows the wordmark colour.** As requested, the wordmark in versions 3 and 4 is entirely white or entirely black. If you'd like the cyan i-dot or the navy `#001233` lettering from the v2 primary logo instead, those versions are in `MitFloww-Logo-Bundle-v2`, and they can be added here too.
- **Transparency on upload.** Some platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn profile photos) flatten transparent PNGs onto their own background colour. Use the ready-to-post files there.
- **Check contrast.** Don't put full white or the white-wordmark version on light backgrounds, or full black or the black-wordmark version on dark ones. The contact sheet shows these cases deliberately.
